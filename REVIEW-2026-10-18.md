# Review — 2026-10-18: Automations, Roles & permissions, Staff hours

Read-only review by five parallel passes: automation engine, automation UI, automation API/docs/seed, permission-model
code audit (with the 96 existing permission tests run: all pass), and a live walkthrough of every default role on the
local demo tenant (`demo-salon`, dev-only "View as" pill). Nothing was edited. Paths are under `apps/web/src/` unless
noted; `W` = `server/workflows.ts`, `L` = `lib/workflows-logic.ts`, `A` = `app/app/[slug]/marketing/automations/`.

Severity: **H** = money, data, legal exposure or a role doing what it must not. **M** = wrong behavior a salon will
hit. **L** = polish / hygiene.

### 1d. Missing capabilities (vs Vagaro / GlossGenius / Fresha / Boulevard)

- Triggers: appointment confirmed, checked-in, deposit/payment received, rescheduled (as its own trigger), package or
  membership expiring / sessions low / renewed, gift card purchased, waitlist opening, review posted publicly, tag
  added, points threshold. Clients created by import, API or inbound text never fire `client_created`
  (`server/import.ts:136,250`, `api/v1/clients/route.ts:38`, `server/inbound.ts:276`).
- Flow control: no branches or step conditions, no "stop if client replies", no exit on review; `exitOnBooking` is the
  only exit and is workflow-wide.
- Sending: no per-client frequency cap across workflows, no per-workflow opt-out, no per-workflow send window or
  weekday, no "business hours only", no per-provider sender (from-number is per location), no MMS on workflow texts
  (`media` exists on notifications, no step field), no owner control of marketing vs transactional classification.
- Templates cheap to add with existing triggers/filters: cancelled → rebook nudge, happy reviewers → Google (review +
  minRating), form-submitted acknowledgement, 90/180-day lapsed ladder, "thanks for your first visit", per-provider
  reminder. Need backend first: deposit/card-on-file reminder, "forms still outstanding" pre-visit nudge, package or
  membership expiring, gift card bought, waitlist opening, no-show-fee warning (no fee context in vars).
- Ops: send-me-a-test, projected sends, per-workflow delivered/opened stats, pause/resume one run, manual enrol.

## Part 3 — Staff hours page

- **Week only.** `?week=` picks a Monday; prev/next move by 7 days; `buildCoverage` takes one `weekStart`
  (`staff-hours/page.tsx:36-38,93-96`, `server/coverage.ts`). No month view, no day view, no date picker.
- **Non-provider staff are invisible.** People, the Edit-hours menu, the time-off form and the coverage query all
  filter `takesAppointments = true` (`staff-hours/page.tsx:43`, `staff-hours/time-off/page.tsx:18`,
  `server/coverage.ts:58`). Receptionists do have weekly hours (demo "Dana Whitfield · Front Desk" has 5 schedule rows)
  and can get time off from their own Staff → Time off tab, but Staff hours never shows them and the team-wide "Add
  time off" cannot pick them. The empty state says "No providers yet", and the footer says "Click a provider's block".
- **No role or position filter.** Filters are week, location and service category. `staff.position` is free text
  ("Lash Artist", "Receptionist"…), and the login role lives on `member.role` via `staff.userId`, so a "Receptionists
  only" filter has two possible keys: position (every staff row has one, but spelling is free) or login role (only
  staff with a login). Simplest first cut: a People select with "Everyone / Providers / Front desk & other staff"
  driven by `takesAppointments`, then a per-position chip list.
- Suggested shape: a Week / Month toggle next to the week arrows (month = a grid of days with each person's hours or
  off marker, no hour-by-hour lanes), and a People filter that also drives the Edit-hours menu and the time-off list.
